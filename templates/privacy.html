1. Who we are

2. Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Account details: your username and email address, and your password, which we store only as a salted one-way hash (scrypt). We cannot see or recover your password.
  • API key names you choose when creating keys.
  • Billing details, if you add them for your invoices: business name, GSTIN, state and billing address.
  • Messages you send us, such as support or refund requests.

Information from Google or GitHub, if you sign in with them

  • Google: your name, email address, whether Google has verified it, and your Google account ID (the standard “openid”, “email” and “profile” permissions).
  • GitHub: your username, GitHub account ID and your primary verified email address (the “read:user” and “user:email” permissions).

We use these only to create your account and sign you in. We never receive your Google or GitHub password, and the access tokens they issue are used once during sign-in and not stored.

Information created when you use the service

  • API keys: a one-way hash of each key and its first 12 characters (so you can tell keys apart), its name, creation date and whether it is active or revoked.
  • API usage records: for each API request, the time, endpoint, status, processing time and, for failures, the type of error. These records never contain the text you sent.
  • Email confirmation: whether you have confirmed your email address. Confirmation links are signed and expire after 3 days; we don’t store them.
  • Plan and quota: your API plan, whether it is a 30-day or yearly plan, how many requests it includes and how many you have used, its start and end dates, your top-up balance, and whether we have sent you a usage warning or renewal reminder for it.
  • Payment records: what you bought, the amount, currency, any coupon and discount, the country used to set the price, Razorpay’s order, payment, subscription and refund IDs, the status and dates. If you turn on auto-renewal, we also keep Razorpay’s subscription ID and its status (we never see your card or UPI details).
  • Invoices: for each purchase, the invoice number and date, what was bought, the amounts and taxes, and the name, email address and billing details on your account at the time.
  • Referrals: your referral code, which account referred you (if any), the accounts that signed up with your link, and the rewards given. People you refer can’t see your details, and you see only counts, not who they are.
  • Security records: the email address and time of failed sign-in attempts, and password-reset codes with their expiry time and number of attempts.
  • Admin actions: when our staff change an account in our admin panel (for example to grant requests or issue a refund), we record who did it, what they did and when.

Technical information

  • IP address: used in memory to apply rate limits and to find your country for regional pricing, using a database stored on our own server. We do not save your IP address in our database; only the country code is saved, and only with a purchase.
  • Server logs: our hosting provider records technical request data such as IP address, time, page requested and browser type, which we use for security and troubleshooting. Logs never include the text you submit or your password.

3. The text you rewrite

Text you send to the editor or the API is processed in our server’s memory to produce the result and is discarded as soon as the response is sent. It is never written to our database or logs, never used to train any model, and never shared with anyone. If something fails while processing, our error logs record only the type of error and where in our code it happened, not its message, because messages could quote your text.

To let you reopen recent work, the editor saves your six most recent results (the original and rewritten text) in your own browser’s local storage. This stays on your device, and you can delete it with “Clear history” in the editor or through your browser settings.

9. Security

  • All connections are encrypted with HTTPS.
  • Passwords are stored as salted scrypt hashes and API keys as SHA-256 hashes; neither can be read back.
  • Sign-in, sign-up, password reset and the API are rate limited, and accounts lock temporarily after repeated wrong passwords.
  • Pages are protected with security headers that block framing and restrict which scripts may run.
  • Access to production systems is limited to the people who run the service.

No system is perfectly secure. If a personal data breach affects you, we will notify you and the relevant authorities as the law requires.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you have some or all of these rights over your personal data:

  • Access: get a summary or copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correction: have inaccurate or incomplete data corrected or updated.
  • Deletion: have your account and personal data erased, subject to records we must keep by law.
  • Portability: receive your data in a structured, commonly used format.
  • Withdraw consent: stop using the service and ask us to delete your account at any time.
  • Objection and restriction (EU and UK): object to processing based on legitimate interests, or ask us to limit it.
  • Nomination (India): nominate someone to exercise your rights if you die or become unable to.
  • Grievance redressal: raise a complaint with our Grievance Officer, and escalate it to the Data Protection Board of India, or to your local data protection authority in the EU or UK.
  • California residents: the rights to know, delete and correct, and not to be discriminated against for using them. We do not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined in California law.
